FOR STUDENTS

Turn curiosity into a question.

Interests in psychology, biology, information science and engineering offer different starting points.

FOLLOW YOUR CURIOSITY

Look closely.
Ask what could explain it.

A small surprise in an animal's behavior can become the beginning of a research question.

01

Build and test questions

Read evidence, consider alternative explanations and develop questions that observation or experiments can address.

02

Reason with data

Use behavioral records, visualization and statistical analysis to understand both findings and their limits.

03

Connect disciplines

Link biological questions with technology, and communicate methods and results clearly.

GRADUATE STUDY

Your route to graduate study

  1. Discuss your interests and intended start date

    Contact Fumihiro Kano with your research interests, relevant experience and intended start date.

    Email Fumihiro Kano ↗
  2. Confirm supervision and the application route

    Review the Graduate School of Human-Environment Studies at Kyushu University and discuss suitable supervision arrangements and the appropriate selection category. Nationality alone does not determine the application route; check the requirements for your degree and field.

    Department of Behavioral and Health Sciences, Psychology course (Japanese) ↗
  3. Apply formally through the Graduate School

    Follow the latest official guidelines for eligibility, documents, selection procedures and deadlines. Prior contact does not guarantee admission or acceptance into the lab.
